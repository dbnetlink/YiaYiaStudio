The Space

Sonically separated, visually connected.

Wood Room

Our flagship recording space. Come in and hear for yourself the smooth buttery diffusion achieved with our unique wood panelling, revealing a rich clarity and depth.

Stone Room

High ceilings and a flagstone floor offer a big reverb, tamed by acoustic panels. The acoustic panels are removable for that big room sound. A large multi-use recording space.

Vocal Booth

Fully padded with acoustic foam panels, this room offers neutral recordings free from any room acostics.

Amp Shack

Small but mighty, this is the key to our live recordings, isolating the sound of bassy amps from intricate instruments.

The Lounge and Yard

Kick back with a pint of home-brewed beer and a great vinyl collection in our cosy lounge, or pass the time with a coffee. Grab some fresh air in the Yard. Enjoy the sunshine! Or failing that (this is England after all) we have a canopy amd some comfy garden furniture. A lovely place for a comfy time-out.
